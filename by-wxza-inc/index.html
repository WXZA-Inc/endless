About Endless

Endless is the public face of WZXA, a background companion concept built by WXZA Inc. The repository describes it in its own words: a theme that breathes, an autonomous companion living quietly in the background of your device, aging when you age, vibing when you vibe, doing the thing you should be doing when you are not. Its makers state the thesis plainly: not an app, the wallpaper.

Every surface on the site subscribes to one global breath clock, an eighteen second cycle that moves through hello, between, harvest and fold, so the landing page, the interactive lockscreen prototype, the generative breath loop and the three-scene meditation all share a single rhythm. Its canonical home is endless.wxza.net, where the companion keeps breathing in public.

About WXZA Inc.

Endless exists because WXZA Inc. keeps a laboratory alongside its production line. WXZA Inc. is a venture studio in Ottawa, Canada, founded in 2023 by Loïc Hazoume, building digital products across wealth, health, real estate, education, commerce and artificial intelligence. Its method carries a name, The Sentient Venture OS: one studio acting as the operating system for everything it creates, so that lessons, infrastructure and standards move freely from one venture to the next instead of being rebuilt each time.

Most of the studio's output is built for users and markets; its flagship is DomainEx, and its creative arm, Atable Studio, shapes the network's design and web work. Endless is the other kind of output, a concept experience with no customer and no pitch, kept deliberately lean. A studio that only ships what is already understood stops learning; the breathing companion is WXZA's way of keeping a live question open in public, on the record, under the studio's own name.

That public attribution matters here more than anywhere. An experiment about a companion that lives in the background of a device invites the obvious question: whose companion, whose responsibility? The answer is printed on the work. WXZA Inc., federally incorporated in Canada in October 2025, stands behind it, and its founder, Loïc Hazoume, is named on the property itself. Every road from this page leads back to the hub in Ottawa, to the founder page, and to the studio's profiles, so a reader, a partner or a search engine can verify the maker without detective work.
